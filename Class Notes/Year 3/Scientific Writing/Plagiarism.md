### Academic Integrity
Definition: The expectation that all members of the academic community act with **honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility**
Regulation (at USTH):
- Applied to both students and lecturers/researcher
- **The percentage of identity for both plagiarism and self-plagiarism is often set at 20%**
	- **The percentage of identity**: The % of similarity
